Office handbook (sample)

Opening hours
The office opens at 8:00. It closes at 19:00 Monday to Thursday and at 18:00 on Fridays. It is closed on weekends and public holidays.

Travel
Train travel is booked in second class. Flights need approval from the team lead before booking. Hotel costs are refunded up to 140 euros a night.

Expenses
Receipts are submitted within 30 days. Meals on business trips are refunded up to 35 euros a day.

Holidays
Every employee has 28 days of paid holiday a year. Requests go to the team lead at least two weeks ahead.

Every name and number in this handbook is made up.
