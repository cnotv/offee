Offee Source code

Your paperwork, done on your own computer.

Offee fills in forms, drafts replies, translates letters and sums up meetings. The AI doing it runs on your Mac or Linux PC, so your passport scan, payslip or contract never goes to someone else's server.

Download the Mac preview Get it for Linux How it works

The source code is public. Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon or a Linux PC (x64 or arm64), and 16 GB of memory. An early test build.

How it works

A locked office inside your computer: Offee works only on what you hand it, and has no line to the outside unless you open one.

Try the switch

Nothing leaves your computer. Every answer comes from the AI on your computer. This is how Offee starts, every time.

Each request waits for you. When a task could use an online AI, Offee shows exactly what would be sent. You say yes or no, one request at a time.

Requests go out for a few minutes, then it seals itself again. Each one is still written down, so you can see later what left.

Where your information lives

Why not just use a search engine or an online AI?

Question An online AI or search engine Offee
Where your documents go To the company's servers, under its rules, sometimes used to train its next model. Nowhere. They stay on your computer unless you let one request out.
What it knows The web. Not your rental contract or last week's meeting. The files you gave it, and it shows which one each answer came from.
What you get Text to copy and paste into the form yourself. The form itself, filled in and, if you like, signed.
What it needs An account and an internet connection. No account. Once installed, no internet.
Checking its work You trust what you are shown. Every task is logged: what went in, which AI answered, whether anything left.

The honest trade: an AI small enough for a laptop is slower than the big online ones and can be wrong. That is why Offee always shows its suggestions for you to check first.

What it does today

Fill and sign forms

Open a PDF form and pick who you are filling it as. Offee fills in what it knows, you check each value, and it saves the PDF, signed if you want.

Read a document into a profile

Give it an ID, a CV or a scan of one, and your details land in a profile for you to check, ready for the next form.

Reply to emails and chats

Drop in a thread and say what you want to answer. You get a draft in your name and tone, based on your own documents.

Translate

A whole document, paragraph by paragraph, keeping the names, numbers and terms you care about.

Sum up a meeting

From notes or a recording: a summary, the decisions, and who does what by when.

Get it

On a Mac

  1. Download Offee-arm64.dmg from the Mac preview release and drag Offee to Applications.
  2. The preview is not notarized yet, so the first time right-click Offee and choose Open. On macOS 15, open it once, then allow it in System Settings, Privacy and Security.
  3. Choose Set up Offee. It downloads the AI (about 10 GB) once, then opens. From then on it needs no internet.

On Linux

  1. Make sure virtualization is on (/dev/kvm exists), then install Colima and the Docker command line tools with Compose.
  2. Download the package for your computer from the Linux preview release: Offee-linux-x64 for most PCs, Offee-linux-arm64 for ARM ones.
  3. On Debian or Ubuntu, install the .deb with sudo apt install ./Offee-linux-x64.deb. On other distributions, unpack the .tar.gz and follow the README.
  4. Open Offee from your applications and choose Set up Offee. If your desktop shows no tray icon, open Offee again to bring its window back.

Then create a profile, or let Offee read one from a document, and try a form. Two made up documents to practise with are in the demo folder.
