This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies: its text, styles, images and videos all come from the same address. There is no form, so it receives nothing you type.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver the pages, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and the usual request details (the page asked for, time, browser) and may keep them in their logs for security. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running a website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. GitHub explains its handling in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to github.com, the download buttons among them, take you to GitHub's own site, where its privacy statement applies.

The Offee app

The app keeps everything on your own computer, or on the server you run it on, and sends nothing to its makers. It only sends a request to an online AI provider when you have set it up to and approved that request, and then it records the request in its run log.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of data about you, restrict or object to its processing, and complain to a data protection authority. Since this site itself stores nothing about you, requests about server logs go to GitHub; anything else to the maintainer through the issue tracker.
